# Sourdough starter — feeding notes

A sourdough starter is a flour-and-water culture kept alive by regular feeding. Held at room temperature (about 22 °C) it needs feeding once a day; kept in the fridge it needs feeding about once a week.

The usual feeding ratio is 1 part starter, 1 part water, 1 part flour by weight. A starter is ready to bake with when it has doubled in size, usually 4 to 6 hours after feeding at room temperature.

A starter that smells like acetone is hungry, not spoiled; feed it twice in one day. Pink or orange streaks mean contamination and the starter should be thrown away.
